# Swapfiets Tech 🚲

Welcome to the GitHub home of **Swapfiets** engineering, data, and AI teams.

Swapfiets is a subscription service for bikes and e-bikes: for a fixed monthly fee, members get an always-working bike, with free repairs whenever they need them. Behind that simple promise sits the software, data platform, and machine learning that keep our fleet rolling and our members happy.

This organization is where our teams build and collaborate on the technology that powers the Swapfiets experience.

## 🔒 A note on our repositories

Most of our work lives in **private repositories** and isn't publicly visible. This page is simply a hello from the teams behind the scenes.
